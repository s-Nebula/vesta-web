Installation

Vesta is distributed as APK files. Android asks for permission to install apps from outside Google Play.

  1. 1Download the APK from the downloads page of this site.
  2. 2Open the file. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” only for the browser or file manager you are using.
  3. 3Install Vesta Parent on the adult's phone and Vesta Tracker on the minor's.
  4. 4When you are done, you can turn that permission off again.

Only download Vesta from the official channels and, if you like, compare the SHA-256 fingerprint published on the downloads page.

Signing in

Vesta Parent supports email and password, Google and GitHub.

  1. 1Check that the email is spelled correctly and that you are online.
  2. 2If you forgot your password, use “Forgot your password?”.
  3. 3If you created the account with Google or GitHub, sign in with the same method.

Vesta support will never ask for your password or secret codes.

Linking

The linking code (QR or 10 characters) is single-use and expires after 24 hours.

  1. 1In Vesta Tracker, tap “Scan QR code” or type the full code.
  2. 2Accept the permissions Vesta Tracker asks for.
  3. 3If the code expired, tap the profile on the Vesta Parent dashboard and then “New code”.
  4. 4Vesta Parent detects the link on its own; there is no need to refresh.

Connection

If Vesta Tracker goes more than 20 minutes without sending data, you will get a “Device offline” alert.

  1. 1Check that the minor's phone is on, charged and online.
  2. 2Check that the phones are still linked.
  3. 3Review Vesta Tracker's battery and background settings in Android.

Some manufacturers stop background apps to save battery. While offline, Vesta Tracker keeps applying the rules for up to 24 hours.

Permissions

Each feature needs an Android permission, as well as the purpose being authorised in Vesta Parent.

  1. 1Usage statistics and limits: Settings → Usage access → Vesta Tracker.
  2. 2App blocking, study mode and limits: Settings → Accessibility → Vesta Tracker → turn on.
  3. 3Web filter and safe search: accept Vesta Tracker's VPN connection request.
  4. 4Notifications: allow them for Vesta Parent to receive alerts and requests.

Location and zones

The location may look out of date for reasons outside the app.

  1. 1Check that GPS is on in the minor's phone.
  2. 2Check that Vesta Tracker has precise location and “Allow all the time”.
  3. 3Confirm that the “Location and history” purpose is authorised.
  4. 4For zones, use a radius of at least 150 metres: GPS has a margin of error.

Privacy

You can access, update, correct or delete data and withdraw authorisations.

  1. 1Review and withdraw purposes in Vesta Parent → “Settings” → “Privacy and authorisations”.
  2. 2For formal enquiries or complaints, write to the privacy channel.
  3. 3Enquiries are answered within 10 business days and complaints within 15, according to the Policy.

Need more detail? The Troubleshooting section of the user guide also covers alerts that don't arrive, study mode and limits, apps or sites that aren't blocked, and services that stop.

Frequently asked questions

What is Vesta Security?

Two Android apps that help protect a minor's digital life. The responsible adult uses Vesta Parent and the minor has Vesta Tracker on their phone.

Vesta supports responsible supervision, but it does not replace family support or emergency services.

How are Vesta Parent and Vesta Tracker different?

Vesta Parent is the management app: profiles, filters, study mode, limits, safe zones, requests, alerts, reports and authorisations.

Vesta Tracker is installed on the minor's phone: it sends the authorised information, applies the rules and lets the minor ask for permission or send an SOS alert.

How they work together

How do I link the phones?

When you create the minor's profile, Vesta Parent shows a QR code and a 10-character code. In Vesta Tracker, scan the QR code or type the code.

The code is single-use and expires after 24 hours. If it expires, tap the profile on the dashboard and then “New code”.

Guide: linking

What can the guardian see?

Depending on the authorised purposes: the last location, arrivals at and departures from safe zones, phone status, app usage, rules and alerts.

Vesta does not read messages, passwords, photos or the content of other apps, and it does not store browsing history.

What happens if the minor's phone goes offline?

Vesta Tracker keeps applying the latest rules for 24 hours and stores the data to send it once reconnected. Study mode and limits work offline.

If the phone goes more than 20 minutes without sending data, the guardian gets an alert.

How do study mode and time limits work?

Study mode only lets the apps you choose open (plus phone, messages, settings and keyboard), for a while or on a schedule. Limits set a daily maximum per app.

If the minor needs more time, they can ask from Vesta Tracker and you approve with one tap.

See study mode

How are purposes authorised?

In Vesta Parent, under “Settings” → “Privacy and authorisations”. Each purpose (location, device status, usage statistics, app control, website filtering and alerts) is independent and optional.

Legal authorisation is separate from Android permissions: granting a system permission does not replace the authorisation to process the data.

How do I withdraw an authorisation?

From “Privacy and authorisations” in Vesta Parent you can withdraw one purpose or all of them. The related features stop working within a minute.

You can also request withdrawal or deletion of data by writing to the privacy channel in the Policy.

Policy: withdrawal

What do I need to install the apps?

Two phones with Android 7.0 or later, an Internet connection and an account for Vesta Parent. Some features need location, usage access, accessibility or local VPN permissions on the minor's phone.

Vesta is only available for Android.
