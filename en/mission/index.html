About us

Looking after them without turning care into surveillance.

Why Vesta exists, where it is heading and the principles behind every product decision.

Origin

A project by Nebula, operated by FORBIDDEN S.A.S.

Vesta Security is a project by Nebula, operated by FORBIDDEN S.A.S. from Santa Marta, Colombia.

It started with a simple question: can you look after your child's phone without spying on them? That is where its defining decisions come from: each feature is authorised separately, the minor can see what is shared and ask for permission, and the limits are written down in public documents.

Mission

Give families clear tools to support the digital lives of children and teenagers, with supervision that is proportionate, authorised and explained, and that respects their privacy and autonomy.

Vision

Be the digital protection tool that parents and guardians trust because they understand what it does, what data it handles and where its limits are.

Values

Principles you can see in the app.

  • Proportionality

    Only what the guardian authorises is turned on, and each feature has a specific purpose. Supervision should serve the minor's interests, not distrust.

  • Transparency

    The terms, privacy policy and guide are published in full, with version and date. We also explain what Vesta cannot guarantee.

  • The minor's voice

    Vesta Tracker tells the minor what is shared and lets them ask for permission or more time. The Terms prohibit hiding the supervision where that would infringe their rights.

  • Control and withdrawal

    Every authorisation can be withdrawn from Vesta Parent, and data subjects can access, correct or ask for their data to be deleted.

  • Only what is needed, only for a while

    Only what each purpose needs is collected, and it is kept for set periods: 90 days for location history and reports, 12 months for alerts.

  • Technical honesty

    No tool blocks everything or locates a phone at every moment. We document the limits so every family can decide with full information.

Privacy

What we do and don't do with data

  • We do not use data for advertising or sell personal profiles.
  • Authorisation to process data is separate from Android permissions.
  • Any new purpose is disclosed and, where required, authorisation is requested before it is turned on.
  • Data subjects can submit enquiries and complaints to FORBIDDEN S.A.S. and then to Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.
Privacy policy

Responsible use

What Vesta is for, and what it is not

  • Vesta is for the family protection of minors. It must not be used to monitor partners, employees or other adults without their consent.
  • Vesta is not an emergency service: the SOS button alerts the family, not the police or medical services.
