How they relate

Two apps that work together.

Vesta Parent is the responsible adult's app, and Vesta Tracker is installed on the minor's phone. They are linked with a QR code and coordinate through Vesta's servers.
Vesta Parent

Creates profiles and codes, sets rules, authorises purposes, views information and answers requests.

Vesta servers

Vesta platform

Authenticates accounts and phones, links them, stores profiles, rules, alerts and reports, checks authorisations and sends notifications.

Vesta Tracker

Receives rules and authorisations; sends location, status, reports, alerts and requests for the authorised purposes.

For the guardian

Vesta Parent

From here, the parent or legal representative manages the minor's digital protection.

Sign in with: Email and password, Google account, GitHub account.

Profiles and linking
Create and edit profiles, show the linking QR code and unlink phones.
Location and zones
Last position, the real time of the signal, and safe zones with arrival and departure notices.
Phone status
Battery, connection, model and Android version. An alert if it stops reporting.
App usage
Screen time, usage per app, opens, PDF and a weekly summary.
Filters and safe search
Blocked or allowed apps and sites, safe search and age recommendations.
Study mode and limits
Study schedules and a daily maximum per app.
Requests and alerts
Notifications even when the app is closed; approve or reject requests with one tap.
Privacy
Consent, authorised purposes and withdrawal.

For the minor's phone

Vesta Tracker

Sends only authorised information, applies the rules and gives the minor a voice.
Linking with QR
Scans the code from Vesta Parent or accepts the typed code.
Authorised location
Background GPS, only if the purpose is authorised. When offline, it stores and sends later.
Rules on the phone
App blocking, study mode and limits (accessibility); web filter and safe search (local VPN).
SOS button
An emergency alert to the guardians with the current location.
Ask for permission
Request more time or to unlock an app, and see the answer.
What Vesta shares
Tells the minor what is and is not shared, and what is active.

Getting started

Five steps, two phones.

  1. 1

    Install Vesta Parent

    On your phone. Create an account with email, Google or GitHub and accept the documents.

    Vesta Parent
  2. 2

    Create the minor's profile

    Name, age and a colour. Vesta Parent shows you a QR code for linking.

    Vesta Parent
  3. 3

    Link their phone

    Install Vesta Tracker on the minor's phone, scan the QR code and grant the permissions.

    Vesta Tracker
  4. 4

    Choose what to turn on

    Authorise only the features you will use. You can withdraw any of them at any time.

    Vesta Parent
  5. 5

    Set the rules

    Filters, study mode, daily limits and safe zones, suited to their age.

    Vesta Parent

Requirements and permissions

What Android asks for, and why.

Two phones with Android 7.0 or later and an Internet connection. Each system permission is requested for a specific feature and does not replace the purpose's authorisation.

Limits

What Vesta cannot guarantee.

We say it plainly, as the Terms and Conditions do.

Terms, section 8
  • Permanent real-time location or absolute accuracy.
  • That every alert arrives instantly.
  • Blocking every site or app: some browsers or unknown encrypted DNS providers can bypass the filter.
  • Working on every phone, browser or Android version.
  • Preventing uninstallation or tampering with the operating system.
  • Replacing emergency services: the SOS button alerts the family, not emergency services.
