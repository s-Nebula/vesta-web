Delete account

Delete your Vesta account and its data.

Vesta Parent and Vesta Tracker are Vesta Security apps, operated by FORBIDDEN S.A.S.. You can delete your account from the app in a minute, or ask for it by e-mail without having the app installed.

From the app (immediate)

  1. 1Open Vesta Parent and go to Settings.
  2. 2In the Account section, tap “Delete account”.
  3. 3Read what is deleted, then confirm your password or sign in again with Google or GitHub.
  4. 4Tick the confirmation box and tap “Delete account permanently”.

The deletion happens straight away and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

Without the app (by e-mail)

Write to nebulagnu@proton.me

  1. 1Send the e-mail from the address you created the account with, so we can check it is yours.
  2. 2Use the subject “Delete my Vesta account” and include the account's e-mail address.
  3. 3We will confirm the deletion within 15 business days at most.

We will never ask for your password or secret codes.

What is deleted

  • Your account, e-mail address, username and notification preferences.
  • Profiles where you are the only guardian, with their location and history, alerts, usage reports, rules, safe zones, study mode, limits and requests.
  • The link between those profiles and their phones: Vesta Tracker stops sending data and can be uninstalled.
  • Your privacy consents and authorisations.

What is kept

  • Profiles you share with another guardian remain for them; only your access is removed.
  • An anonymous record that the deletion took place (date and number of profiles), without your e-mail, name or any identifier of yours.
  • Backups are deleted or anonymised on their cycle, as the privacy policy describes.

What about Vesta Tracker?

Vesta Tracker has no account of its own: it depends on the profile created in Vesta Parent. When the guardian's account is deleted, or the phone is unlinked from Vesta Parent, it stops sending data.

More detail in the Privacy Policy.
