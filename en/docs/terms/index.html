3. Description of the service

Vesta is a technology solution made up of two Android applications:

  • Vesta Parent: lets the administrator manage profiles, linked devices and protection rules, and view the information received.
  • Vesta Tracker: collects and transmits authorised information from the device, applies rules and runs protection services.

Its features include:

  1. Linking devices with a code or a QR code.
  2. Viewing location.
  3. Available location history.
  4. Alerts about battery, speed, blocks, safe zones, loss of connection and protections being turned off.
  5. Aggregated supervision of app usage.
  6. App blocking.
  7. Domain filtering through accessibility and a DNS VPN.
  8. Management of block and exception rules.
  9. Privacy management and requests related to personal data.
  10. Safe zones with arrival and departure notices.
  11. Emergency (SOS) button in Vesta Tracker.
  12. Study mode, activated manually or on a schedule.
  13. Daily time limits per app.
  14. Requests from the minor to the administrator for temporary permissions.
  15. Safe search on compatible search engines and blocking of known encrypted DNS providers.
  16. Weekly activity summary.
  17. An information section for the minor about the data being shared.

The availability of each feature depends on the version, device compatibility, permissions, connectivity and settings.

7. Operating system permissions

Vesta requires Android permissions for certain features.

The user understands that:

  • Location may require precise and background access.
  • Usage statistics require specific authorisation and are also used to apply daily time limits.
  • Accessibility makes it possible to detect window events and read the address bar of a compatible browser.
  • The local VPN processes DNS queries in order to apply filters.
  • Notifications and foreground services are used to report which features are active.
  • The camera is used in Vesta Tracker only to read the linking QR code. Images are neither stored nor transmitted.

Revoking permissions may prevent or limit the related features.

Vesta does not use permissions for purposes other than those described in its Privacy Policy.

8. Service limitations

Vesta is a support tool and does not guarantee:

  • Permanent real-time location.
  • Absolute accuracy of coordinates.
  • Uninterrupted availability.
  • Immediate delivery of alerts.
  • Blocking of every website or app.
  • Compatibility with every device, browser or Android version.
  • Prevention of every form of access to unwanted content.
  • Protection against tampering with, uninstalling or altering the operating system.

Location and reports may be delayed by low battery, GPS signal, connectivity, manufacturer restrictions or system conditions.

DNS filtering may not detect traffic that uses alternative resolution mechanisms, encrypted DNS, apps with direct connections or other methods that bypass the filtering mechanism.

Blocking of encrypted DNS providers is limited to the providers known to Vesta and may not prevent other resolution mechanisms.

Safe search only applies to compatible search engines and services, and depends on each provider's configuration.

Safe zone notices depend on GPS accuracy and connectivity: they may be delayed, occur away from the exact edge of the zone, or not occur at all.

Study mode and time limits do not block calls, SMS messages, system settings or the keyboard, and may be affected if the accessibility service is turned off.

When the device is offline, Vesta Tracker keeps the latest rules received for a limited time and sends pending data once the connection is restored.

Vesta is not an emergency response service, a police location service or a substitute for medical, security or child protection services. The SOS button notifies the administrators when there is a connection, but it does not contact emergency services. In an emergency, call 123 or the relevant emergency service.

11. Unlinking, termination and deletion

The administrator may unlink a device when the feature is available.

Linking codes and QR codes expire after 24 hours. The administrator can generate a new one at any time, which invalidates the previous one.

Unlinking ends the active association, but does not in itself delete historical data.

The user may request the deletion of personal data in accordance with the Privacy Policy, unless a legal obligation or exception justifies keeping it.

Deletion covers, as applicable:

  • The minor's profile.
  • Location history.
  • Usage reports.
  • Alerts.
  • Protection rules.
  • Safe zones, study mode settings and time limits.
  • The minor's requests and weekly summaries.
  • Device records.
  • Data associated with the account.

Backup copies will be deleted according to the established retention cycle, except for legal obligations or legitimate security needs.
