Location

Vesta Tracker gets the phone's coordinates from Android's GPS and sends them to the platform. Vesta Parent shows the last position on the map, the time it was taken, and a notice if the signal is more than 15 minutes old.

What it is for
Know where the phone was at the last report and, if needed, open directions to that point in Google Maps with the “Directions” button.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Parent, open the minor's profile.
  2. 2.Go to the “Map” tab.
  3. 3.Check the time of the “Last signal” before reading the position.
Where it is managed
Viewed in Vesta Parent. Collected by Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Purpose: Location and historyAndroid: Precise location · Background location · Foreground service

Limits. The frequency depends on GPS, battery, connection and manufacturer restrictions. When offline, positions are stored on the phone and sent once it reconnects.

New in 1.1

Safe zones

The guardian marks a point and a radius on the map (from 50 to 2,000 metres) and chooses a notice on arrival, on departure or both. Vesta Tracker compares its position with the zones on the phone itself, so the notice is generated even if the connection comes and goes.

What it is for
Know the minor got to school or left home without having to watch the map.
How to use it
  1. 1.On the profile screen, tap “Zones” and then “New zone”.
  2. 2.Tap the map to set the centre and adjust the radius.
  3. 3.Enter a name, choose the notices and save.
Where it is managed
Set up in Vesta Parent. Detected by Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Purpose: Location and history

Limits. It depends on GPS accuracy: the notice may arrive late or happen slightly outside the exact edge. To avoid false notices, a departure only counts once the phone moves beyond a margin.

New in 1.1

SOS button

In Vesta Tracker, the minor presses and holds the “Emergency” button. Their guardians instantly receive a critical alert with the current location, even if they chose to receive only important notices.

What it is for
Let the minor ask their family for help with a single gesture.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Tracker, press and hold the “Emergency” button.
  2. 2.In Vesta Parent, tap the notification and then “See where they are now”.
Where it is managed
Used in Vesta Tracker. Received in Vesta Parent.
Authorisation
Purpose: Alerts and eventsAndroid: The location is attached only if its purpose is authorised

Limits. The SOS button alerts the family, not emergency services. In an emergency, call 123. When offline, the alert is sent once the connection is restored.

Device status

Vesta Parent shows the battery level, whether the phone is charging, the connection type, the manufacturer, the model and the Android version. If Vesta Tracker goes more than 20 minutes without sending data, the server alerts the guardian, even when Vesta Parent is closed.

What it is for
Check that the phone is still on, connected and protected.
How to use it
  1. 1.Open the minor's profile in Vesta Parent.
  2. 2.Go to the “Device” tab.
Where it is managed
Viewed in Vesta Parent.
Authorisation
Purpose: Device status

Usage statistics

With Android's usage access, Vesta Tracker generates activity reports. Vesta Parent shows them by day or by week: screen time, usage per app, opens and daily activity. Every Sunday evening a weekly summary arrives too.

What it is for
See how screen time is spread and talk about it as a family with real data. Reports can be exported as PDF.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Tracker, grant usage access from Android settings.
  2. 2.In Vesta Parent, open the “Reports” tab.
  3. 3.Choose “Last 7 days” or “Last day” and, if needed, tap “Export PDF”.
Where it is managed
Viewed in Vesta Parent. The permission is granted in Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Purpose: App usage statisticsAndroid: Usage access (turned on manually in Settings)
New in 1.1

Study mode

While it is on, the minor's phone only opens the allowed apps, plus the phone, messages, settings and keyboard. It can run for 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or with no limit, or on weekly schedules, including overnight (for example, 21:00 to 07:00).

What it is for
Help focus on homework or switch off at night.
How to use it
  1. 1.On the profile screen, tap “Study mode”.
  2. 2.Turn it on for a while or add a schedule.
  3. 3.Select the apps allowed while studying.
Where it is managed
Set up in Vesta Parent. Applied by Vesta Tracker, even offline.
Authorisation
Purpose: App controlAndroid: Accessibility service

Limits. It works while Vesta Tracker's accessibility service stays on. If it is turned off, the guardian gets an alert.

New in 1.1

Time limits

The guardian gives each app a daily maximum, from 15 minutes to 4 hours. Vesta Tracker measures usage on the phone itself and, when the time runs out, blocks the app until the next day. Vesta Parent shows how much each one has been used today.

What it is for
Set specific limits for games or social media without blocking them completely.
How to use it
  1. 1.On the profile screen, tap “Limits”.
  2. 2.Choose the app.
  3. 3.Select the maximum time per day.
Where it is managed
Set up in Vesta Parent. Applied by Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Purpose: App controlAndroid: Accessibility service · Usage access
New in 1.1

Requests

When Vesta blocks an app, Vesta Tracker shows a notice to ask for permission. The minor chooses the time and can write a message. The guardian gets a notification and approves, choosing the minutes, or rejects. While the permission lasts, the app works even if it is blocked, in study mode or out of daily time.

What it is for
Make limits negotiable without taking the phone away or breaking the rules.
How to use it
  1. 1.The minor taps the block notice or opens “Ask for permission” in Vesta Tracker.
  2. 2.In Vesta Parent, tap the notification or open “Requests” on the profile screen.
  3. 3.Tap “Approve” and choose the minutes, or tap “Reject”.
Where it is managed
Requested in Vesta Tracker. Answered in Vesta Parent.
Authorisation
Purpose: App control

App blocking

Vesta Tracker syncs the list of installed apps. In Vesta Parent you choose which ones to block, and each rule can be turned on or off. If someone tries to open a blocked app, Vesta Tracker goes back to the home screen and offers to ask for permission.

What it is for
Limit access to specific apps according to age and family agreements.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Parent, go to “Filters”.
  2. 2.Select the “Android app” category and tap “Choose installed apps”.
  3. 3.Select the apps and save.
Where it is managed
Set up in Vesta Parent. Applied by Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Purpose: App controlAndroid: Accessibility service

Limits. It depends on the Android version, the manufacturer and the accessibility service staying on. If it is turned off, the guardian gets an alert.

Web filter and safe search

Vesta Parent lets you block or allow specific websites. With safe search, Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and YouTube open in their content-filtered versions. Vesta Tracker also blocks known encrypted DNS providers, so the filter cannot be turned off from the browser. With one tap you can apply the recommendations for the minor's age.

What it is for
Restrict specific sites and their subdomains. If a block rule and an allow rule match, the more specific one wins; in a tie, the allow rule wins.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Parent, go to “Filters”.
  2. 2.Turn on “Safe search” and “Block encrypted DNS”.
  3. 3.Tap “Apply age recommendations” or add sites by hand.
Where it is managed
Set up in Vesta Parent. Applied by Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Purpose: Website filteringAndroid: Local VPN connection (for filtering only) · Accessibility service

Limits. Vesta's VPN is not a commercial VPN and does not hide your IP address. Encrypted DNS blocking is limited to known providers; browsers or apps with their own resolution may bypass the filter.

Alerts and notifications

Vesta Parent receives notifications for: low (20%) and critical (10%) battery, speed above about 80 km/h, blocked apps and sites, arrivals at and departures from safe zones, SOS alerts, the phone going offline, daily time used up, and protections being turned off on the minor's phone.

What it is for
Find out about what matters without opening the app. In Settings you can choose to receive only critical alerts instantly; the rest stay in the app and in the weekly summary.
How to use it
  1. 1.Allow Vesta Parent notifications in Android.
  2. 2.Open the “Alerts” tab to see them, filter them by severity or delete them.
Where it is managed
Received in Vesta Parent. Generated by Vesta Tracker and the server.
Authorisation
Purpose: Alerts and eventsAndroid: Notifications

Limits. Alerts are not an emergency service and may be delayed if the phone is offline. Deleting an alert does not delete the location history or reports.

Profiles and linking

The guardian creates a profile for each minor (name, age and colour) and can edit it later. Vesta Parent shows a QR code and a 10-character code, single-use and valid for 24 hours. Vesta Tracker scans the QR code with the camera or accepts the typed code. When a phone is unlinked, it detects it on its own and turns off its protections.

What it is for
Link each phone to its profile in a controlled way, and remove it when no longer needed.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Parent, tap “Add device” and fill in the profile.
  2. 2.In Vesta Tracker, tap “Scan QR code” and point at the code.
  3. 3.To edit or unlink, open the profile's ⋮ menu on the dashboard.
Where it is managed
Vesta Parent and Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
Android: Camera (only to read the QR code; optional)

Limits. Unlinking a phone does not, by itself, delete historical data.

Privacy and authorisations

Before using the dashboard, the guardian accepts the legal documents, declares their legal representation and states whether the minor was informed and heard. Then they authorise, one by one, the purposes they want to turn on. In Vesta Tracker, the “What Vesta shares” section explains to the minor, in plain language, what is shared and what is not.

What it is for
Process each type of information only with express authorisation, which can be withdrawn without affecting the rest.
How to use it
  1. 1.In Vesta Parent, open “Settings” and then “Privacy and authorisations”.
  2. 2.Review the consent and the versions of the documents accepted.
  3. 3.Withdraw one purpose or all of them at once.
Where it is managed
Managed in Vesta Parent. Respected by Vesta Tracker.
Authorisation
No specific purpose required.
